Identify Operational Gaps

Our cybersecurity consulting services help network owners, CISOs, IT leaders, and mission owners identify operational gaps in their cyber posture using the DOTMLPF-P framework.

We don't just look at technology—we evaluate your entire cybersecurity ecosystem across doctrine, organization, training, materiel, leadership, personnel, facilities, and policy.

Our Consulting Services

  • Cyber Readiness Assessments - Comprehensive gap analysis across all DOTMLPF-P domains
  • On-Prem, Cloud, and Hybrid Assessments - Evaluate your entire infrastructure
  • Cyber Range Design and Implementation - Build training and testing environments
  • SOC and Enterprise Architecture Modernization - Design next-generation security operations
  • Compliance and Accreditation Support - Navigate NIST, CMMC, FedRAMP, and other frameworks

The DOTMLPF-P Advantage

Most cybersecurity assessments focus only on technology. We take a holistic approach:

  • Doctrine - Are your policies and procedures aligned with best practices?
  • Organization - Is your structure optimized for effective cybersecurity?
  • Training - Do your teams have the skills they need?
  • Materiel - Are your tools and platforms effective?
  • Leadership - Is strategic direction clear and actionable?
  • Personnel - Do you have the right people in the right roles?
  • Facilities - Is your infrastructure secure and resilient?
  • Policy - Are governance and compliance frameworks effective?
