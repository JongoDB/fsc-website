Capabilities

Everything your portal does, in one place.

Self-service for the whole billing relationship — invoices, payments, statements, account details — all from any device, anytime.

  • View every invoice — Open, paid, overdue — current balance and due dates at a glance.
  • Accept estimates online — Review proposed engagements and approve quotes. Acceptance converts directly to an invoice.
  • Pay by ACH or card — Stripe-powered checkout. Receipts auto-emailed and archived in the portal.
  • Download statements — Generate full account statements on demand for records, reconciliation, or audit.
  • Keep your details current — Billing contact, business address, tax ID — changes apply to every future invoice.
  • 24/7 self-service — From any device. No call queues, no email back-and-forth for routine billing tasks.

Hosted by Zoho. Payments by Stripe.

Your billing portal is hosted by Zoho Books, our system of record for invoices and statements. Payments are processed by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of payment-card security. FSC never sees or stores your card details.
