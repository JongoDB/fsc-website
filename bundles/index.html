App Bundles

What Are App Bundles?

Pre-curated, documented software stacks designed for specific missions

Our app bundles are pre-configured software stacks that solve specific operational needs. Each bundle includes all the tools, documentation, and deployment guides you need to get up and running quickly.

Bundles are designed to work with our Secure Kubernetes Core platform, but can also be deployed standalone or integrated into existing environments.
