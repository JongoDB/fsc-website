The Bundle

All-in-One Business Operations

The Business Management Suite is a comprehensive, open-source stack designed for startups and small companies. It provides all the tools you need to manage your business operations without expensive SaaS subscriptions.

All applications are pre-configured, integrated, and ready for deployment. Self-host for complete data ownership and privacy, or deploy to your preferred cloud provider.

What's Included

  • CRM — Customer relationship management and sales pipeline
  • Accounting & Invoicing — Financial management, invoicing, and expense tracking
  • Inventory Management — Product and stock management
  • Marketing Automation — Email campaigns, lead nurturing, and analytics
  • HR & Payroll — Employee management and payroll processing
  • Analytics Dashboard — Business intelligence and reporting
Benefits

Benefits

The Business Management Suite offers compelling advantages for growing businesses:

  • Cost-Effective — Eliminate per-user licensing fees and expensive subscriptions
  • Data Ownership — Your business data stays on your infrastructure
  • Integrated — All applications work together with single sign-on
  • Customizable — Open-source tools you can modify for your needs
  • Scalable — Grows with your business from startup to enterprise
  • Compliant — Meet data privacy regulations (, , etc.)

Who It's For

Startups, small businesses, and growing companies that want professional-grade business tools without the enterprise price tag. Ideal for businesses that value data ownership and operational independence.

Bundle Components

Complete business operations stack

Customer Relationship Management

Track leads, manage contacts, automate sales pipelines, and nurture customer relationships with a full-featured CRM.

Accounting & Financial Management

Double-entry accounting, invoicing, expense tracking, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting.

Inventory & Warehouse Management

Track stock levels, manage multiple warehouses, automate reordering, and integrate with sales channels.

Marketing Automation

Email campaigns, landing pages, lead scoring, marketing analytics, and automated workflows.

HR & Employee Management

Employee directory, time tracking, leave management, payroll integration, and performance tracking.

Business Intelligence & Analytics

Custom dashboards, KPI tracking, sales forecasting, and data visualization across all business operations.

Project Management

Task tracking, Gantt charts, team collaboration, and project timelines to keep your operations organized.

Document Management

Centralized document storage, version control, digital signatures, and collaborative editing for business workflows.

Stack

Technology Stack

Built on proven open-source technologies

Core Applications

  • ERPNext / Odoo — Core ERP and business management
  • Invoice Ninja / Akaunting — Invoicing and accounting
  • Monica / EspoCRM — Customer relationship management
  • Mautic — Marketing automation platform
  • Metabase / Apache Superset — Business intelligence

Infrastructure

  • PostgreSQL / MySQL — Relational database
  • Redis — Caching and session management
  • Keycloak — Identity and access management ()
  • Traefik / Nginx — Reverse proxy and load balancing
  • Docker / Kubernetes — Container orchestration
