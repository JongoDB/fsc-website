Who We Are

Operator-led cybersecurity solutions from proven leaders

Operator-Led. Practitioner-First.

Fighting Smart Cyber was founded by three proven cyber operations leaders with decades of combined experience conducting offensive and defensive cyber operations at the highest classification levels.

We're not career theorists. We're operators who've designed, built, and run cyber capabilities in production. Our team includes a retired three-star general with cyber and operational command experience, a CWO3 with deep DCO/OCO expertise, and a senior NCO who's built secure architectures and cyber ranges from the ground up.

We think holistically in DOTMLPF-P terms—understanding that effective cybersecurity requires alignment across:

  • Doctrine - Policies, procedures, and operational frameworks
  • Organization - Structure, roles, and responsibilities
  • Training - Skills development and knowledge transfer
  • Materiel - Tools, platforms, and technology solutions
  • Leadership - Strategic direction and decision-making
  • Personnel - Talent acquisition and team development
  • Facilities - Infrastructure and physical security
  • Policy - Governance, compliance, and regulatory alignment
