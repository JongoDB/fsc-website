Matthew "Jerry" Glavy

Chief Executive Officer

Lieutenant General, U.S. Marine Corps (Retired)

Lieutenant General Glavy brings over 39 years of experience operating in the most challenging domains of air, sea, space, and cyberspace. From numerous deployments with Marine Expeditionary Units around the world to strategic operations at the highest levels of national security, his career represents an unmatched combination of operational credibility and technical expertise.

As Commanding Officer of Marine Helicopter Squadron One (HMX-1), he served as the President's helicopter pilot, ensuring safe and timely movement of the Commander-in-Chief aboard Marine One. As Commander of U.S. Marine Corps Forces Cyberspace Command, he led the transformation of global network operations, commanded Joint Task Force ARES executing offensive cyber operations, oversaw construction of a $200M cyberspace operations facility, and grew the command to a 3,000-person fighting force. He then stood up and led U.S. Marine Corps Forces Space Command, bringing the Marine Corps into the space domain.

His final assignment as Deputy Commandant for Information gave him responsibility for modernizing the Marine Corps Enterprise Network with hybrid cloud infrastructure integrating AI and machine learning capabilities, transforming the ISR enterprise, and managing a $3 billion annual budget. Today, he brings this wealth of operational experience to help organizations fight smarter against evolving cyber threats.
