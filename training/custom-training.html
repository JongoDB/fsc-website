Custom Training Packages

Training Tailored to Your Needs

We can design custom training programs tailored to your specific needs, environment, and skill levels. Whether you need executive cyber risk workshops, technical deep-dives, or comprehensive security team training, we'll create a curriculum that fits.

Our custom training programs are designed by operators who understand real-world requirements. We'll work with you to identify your team's specific needs and create a training program that addresses them directly.

Custom Training Options

  • Executive Workshops - Cyber risk management for leadership
  • Technical Deep-Dives - Advanced topics for experienced teams
  • Comprehensive Programs - Multi-week training for entire security teams
  • Environment-Specific - Training tailored to your specific infrastructure

Our Process

  • Discovery - Understand your organization's needs, environment, and skill levels
  • Curriculum Design - Create a custom curriculum addressing your specific requirements
  • Lab Environment - Build training environments that mirror your infrastructure
  • Delivery - Flexible delivery options: on-site, virtual, or hybrid
  • Assessment - Measure learning outcomes and adjust as needed
  • Follow-Up - Ongoing support and additional training as your needs evolve
